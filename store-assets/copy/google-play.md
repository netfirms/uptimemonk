Install UptimeMonke and know the second your site goes down.

## Data safety questionnaire — answers that match how the app works

- **Does your app collect or share user data?** Yes.
- **Data types collected — Personal info:**
  - *Email address* — collected, not shared, app functionality + account
    management, encrypted in transit.
  - *Name* — collected (from the identity provider), not shared, app
    functionality, encrypted in transit.
- **Data types collected — Device or other IDs:**
  - *Device or other IDs* — the FCM registration token, collected and stored so
    alerts can be delivered to this device. Not shared with third parties for
    advertising. Encrypted in transit.
- **Data types NOT collected:** Location, Financial info, Health, Messages,
  Photos, Contacts, Calendar, Web browsing history, Files and docs, App
  activity, App performance crashes/diagnostics.
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes.
- **Can users request deletion?** Yes — account deletion removes the account
  and its monitors. Provide a deletion request route in the policy.

> These answers must match what the app actually does, not the marketing
> description. The FCM token is the one device identifier the app persists, and
> it goes to the UptimeMonke backend so push alerts reach the right phone.
