## Description  (limit 4000)

```
UptimeMonke watches your websites and APIs around the clock and tells you the
moment something breaks — before your customers do.

Sub-minute checks. Multi-channel alerts. No subscriptions.

WHY UPTIMEMONKE

DOWN BEFORE YOUR USERS NOTICE
Continuous HTTP, SSL, TCP, DNS and ICMP checks run on a global edge fleet. When
a monitor fails, alerts go out immediately over the channels your team already
lives in — Slack, Discord, custom webhooks and email.

EVERY CHECK TYPE, OUT OF THE BOX
• HTTP(S) and API monitors with keyword and status-code assertions
• SSL certificate expiry warnings at 30, 14 and 7 days
• TCP port and DNS record monitoring
• ICMP ping
• Cron heartbeats — alarm when a scheduled job stops calling home

INSTANT, DEPENDABLE ALERTS
Notifications land in Slack, Discord, custom webhooks and email. Alert contacts
are verified before they can receive anything, so nobody can spoof a channel and
page your team.

BRANDED PUBLIC STATUS PAGES
Publish a clean status page with 90-day uptime history and a live incident feed.
Keep customers informed during an outage instead of answering support tickets.

BUILT FOR DEVELOPERS
• Response-time and latency charts across 24h, 7d, 30d and 90-day windows
• Full incident log with resolved outages and causes
• Cross-region confirmation guards against false alarms
• Hardened against SSRF and cloud metadata attacks — safe even for internal
  targets

FREE, FOREVER
Every feature works on a free account: all check types, sub-minute intervals,
public status pages and alerting. There is no paid plan. An optional one-off
donation buys extra check capacity for heavier workloads — and running out
never stops your monitoring.

Download UptimeMonke and know the second your site goes down.
```

## App Review Information — Notes

```
UptimeMonke is an uptime monitoring service. The app is the companion client for
the UptimeMonke platform.

Sign-in is required because monitors are scoped to your account. A reviewer
account is provided below with sample monitors already configured, including a
monitor currently in a DOWN state so the incident view can be evaluated.

No purchase is required to use any feature. The optional donation is a one-time
support payment that increases check capacity; it grants no digital goods or
extra app functionality and unlocks nothing in the UI.
```
